# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Art-healing enthusiasts, prospective students, and existing members use the portal to upload personal artwork,
receive an AI-assisted emotional reflection, revisit reports, and manage their identity. They may arrive in a
vulnerable or contemplative state, so the workflow must feel calm, private, and easy to understand.

## Product Purpose

Original Sense turns a personal artwork into a private, supportive interpretation of its colour, composition,
lines, and emotional qualities. Success means users can move from choosing an image to understanding when their
report will be ready without uncertainty, pressure, or technical friction.

## Brand Personality

Soulful, organic, and luminous. The product should feel like entering an unhurried art sanctuary: painterly,
warm, respectful, and quietly reassuring.

## Anti-references

Avoid clinical health portals, dense admin dashboards, gamified wellness apps, generic SaaS cards, loud AI
spectacle, and interfaces that make private emotional work feel transactional or rushed.

## Design Principles

1. Put the artwork first and keep product controls visually secondary.
2. Make every wait understandable with gentle, specific progress and recovery guidance.
3. Preserve breathing room so emotionally sensitive tasks never feel hurried.
4. Use texture and motion to add warmth, while keeping feedback legible and purposeful.
5. Earn trust through privacy clarity, accessible interaction, and honest system status.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA and strive for AAA contrast on body text. Never rely on colour alone, provide meaningful
image alternatives, retain visible teal focus indicators, and disable or reduce nonessential motion when
`prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled.
